- Image AWT26110003-wb-stepB-be.bin, 1080p60 HDMI stub on D2, reset to mode ACTIVE: **5734 ms** firmware time (Timer2 ms), 76400000 instr.
- Blocking waits measured on that path (delay_ms, poll, wait_event): **1968 ms** in 901 calls = 34 % of the path; the rest is main-loop compute and waiting for timer events / state-machine timers.

## Findings (hand-written, from the table and the sim run)

- Boot to ACTIVE in the sim is 5.7 s; 2.0 s of it is blocking waits (blind spin or poll), 3.7 s is main-loop passes and timer events. Sim hardware is partly modelled: unit 3 time stamps (#40) are the real numbers.
- Boot, reset to first main-loop pass (stage 5, 506 ms): **200 ms blind `PCB_DDR_POWER_RELEASE_TIME` (D026) plus 2 x 10 ms (D027, D028) wait for DDR / VCCK rails our board header never switches** (PCB macros are no-ops). Class removable. The #40 vendor 200 ms (bank 5 d2a0) is a separate vendor-PCB delay.
- Our own: `AwtoDiagSelfTest` 10 ms + 200 x 5 us + bench on every panel-on (#53.6): removable.
- Panel power-on (DPTx): T1 10 ms (spec 0.5-10 ms rail ramp), DPTx PHY init 10 ms, then the T2 HPD poll with a 250 ms bound (spec T2 max 200 ms; exits at HPD), link training with a 5 ms wait after every attempt including the passing one (D017: removable on success).
- **Mode change tears the panel down and up again** (RTD2014UserInterface.c:662 `_BACKLIGHT_OFF`, then the backlight-on timer event): PWM to min + 50 ms (D099) and T4 250 ms (D104) blind (300 ms), DPTx stream disable, display setting, stream enable, then the 550 ms T3 as a deferred event (armed at 5181 ms in the sim, backlight on at 5731 ms), then 50 ms PWM wait (D097). About 350 ms blocking and 550 ms deferred between backlight off and on, on the critical path to the picture. At first power-on the T3 550 ms runs **blind** once (D103, `_BACKLIGHT_ON` branch, fw 1105 ms) and blocks the main loop. Datasheet needs T4 >= 500 ms (video valid to backlight) and T5 >= 100 ms (backlight off to video off) only when the video stream is stopped and restarted; keeping the stream up across a mode change removes both.
- HDMI HPD toggle at search start: 500 ms blind (D083, user setting x 100 ms), blocks the main loop; the HPD-high step can be a deferred event.
- TMDS PreDetect 6 ms (D081, #40) and the other TMDS pre-detect/scan waits are not in the sim budget (the sim stubs `ScalerTMDSNormalPreDetect` / `ScanInputPort` / `StableDetect`); on hardware each search pass pays them per port.
- With a picture up the loop still waits about 0.1 s per second: `ScalerOsdHardwareCompression` 2 ms x ~47/s (D041, OSD HW busy flag exists) plus the offline-measure poll; this is the #40 steady-state wait.
- Interrupt handlers contain blind us delays: `DELAY_30US_EXINT` in the debug (0x3A/0x3C) and DDC-detect EXINT1 handlers (#53.12), ~5.5 ms of `DELAY_XUS` steps in `ScalerTMDSRx2SetPhy_EXINT0` / Rx3 (EXINT0 PHY set).
- Search and wait-to-active constants (D2 HDMI: search 2 s, active wait 100 ms, SDK defaults, no spec) are listed under the constants table; panel T1..T6 map to datasheet T1, T2 (HPD), T4, T5, T6, T8.
